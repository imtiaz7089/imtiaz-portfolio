AI-Based Phishing Email Detector

A machine-learning project that classifies emails as phishing or legitimate using TF-IDF features and Logistic Regression.

Evaluation: 99.45% accuracy, 99.43% precision, 99.59% recall and 99.51% F1-score on a held-out CEAS_08 test split.

These are experimental results, not a guarantee of real-world detection performance.
